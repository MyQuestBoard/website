QuestBoard

Privacy Policy

Updated November 13, 2026

QuestBoard is in testing. This policy describes the app as it works today and will be reviewed before launch. If anything here is unclear, email adamayers@myquestboard.com.

QuestBoard is an app for exploring Ohio and for posting and doing small paid jobs (“quests”). This page explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, what other people can see, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, and your password in scrambled (hashed) form that we can’t read. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link.
  • Your date of birth, asked once before you post, apply, sell or set up payouts, to check you’re 18 or older. Only you can see it.
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to verify it. Others see that it’s verified, never the number.

Your profile and activity

  • Your display name, avatar color, bio and, if you work quests, your worker profile (services, rates and the counties you work in).
  • Quests you post (description, pay, schedule, address, photos), applications, messages, reviews, disputes and reports.
  • Your record on QuestBoard, such as completed quests, no-shows, late cancellations and ratings, which we use to show trust levels.
  • Market listings and messages, and the places you visit, check in to or suggest edits for.
  • Photos you add: of a quest, before and after photos, dispute photos, place photos and listing photos.

Location

  • While you use the app: to show the map, uncover the areas you explore, check you in at places, and find quests near you.
  • Around a quest you’re hired for, if you turn on automatic check-in: with your permission set to “Always”, the app watches for you heading to and arriving at the quest, and leaving, only in a window around the quest’s time. Only “on the way”, “arrived” and “left” are shared with the poster, never where you are.
  • You can turn location off in your phone’s settings. Some features, like check-ins and nearby quests, won’t work without it.

Payments

  • Stripe, our payments partner, handles card details for posters, and identity and bank details for workers who set up payouts. We never see or store full card numbers, bank account numbers or Social Security numbers.
  • We keep records of each payment: amounts, what it was for, its status, refunds, QuestBoard credit and payouts.

Your device

  • A push notification token, so we can send notifications you’ve turned on, and your notification settings.
  • Like most online services, our providers keep short-lived logs of requests to their servers, which include IP addresses.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We don’t read your contacts.
  • We don’t track where you are outside the app, except around a quest you’re hired for, with automatic check-in turned on.

What other people can see

  • Everyone using QuestBoard can see your display name, profile, worker profile, reviews, trust level and the quests or listings you post. Open quests show an approximate area, not the exact address.
  • The person you’re working with sees the quest’s exact address once you’re hired, your messages, the quest’s photos, and check-in updates.
  • Nobody else sees your email address, date of birth, phone number, payment details or exact location.

Why we use it

To run QuestBoard: to sign you in, match posters and workers, process payments and payouts, keep people safe and quests fair (trust records, check-ins, photos, disputes and reports), send the notifications and receipts you’ve asked for, prevent fraud, meet legal requirements such as tax reporting, and answer you when you contact us.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States.
  • Stripe: card payments, worker identity checks and payouts, and tax forms.
  • Google: sign-in, if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Resend: sends our emails (sign-up, password reset and receipts).
  • Expo: delivers push notifications.
  • OpenFreeMap and Photon (Komoot): map tiles and address search. Address search sends what you type when looking up an address.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

They use the information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When it’s deleted, your profile, photos and activity are deleted or made anonymous. We keep some records longer where the law or fairness needs it: payment and tax records (Stripe and we must keep these for several years), and a quest or dispute that someone else is still part of. Those are kept without anything that shows they were yours where we can.

Deleting your account

See how to delete your account. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing adamayers@myquestboard.com from the address on your account.

Age

QuestBoard’s quests, market and payouts are for people 18 and older, and we ask for a date of birth before they’re used. The map and exploring are open to everyone 13 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Information travels between the app and our servers encrypted. Each person can read only their own private information, and every action is checked on our servers.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
