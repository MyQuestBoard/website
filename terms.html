1. About QuestBoard

QuestBoard is an app for exploring Ohio and for connecting people who need small jobs done (“posters”) with people who do them (“workers”). A job posted on QuestBoard is a “quest”. QuestBoard provides the app and handles payments for quests paid in the app. QuestBoard does not do the work itself and does not employ workers.

2. Your account

  • Give accurate information and keep your password safe. You’re responsible for what happens on your account.
  • The map and exploring are for people 13 and older. Quests, the market and payouts are for people 18 and older, and we ask for your date of birth before you use them.
  • One account per person. Don’t use someone else’s account or pretend to be someone you’re not.

3. Workers are independent

Workers are independent contractors, not employees of QuestBoard or of posters. Workers choose which quests to apply to, how to do the work, and when (within what they agree with the poster). Workers are responsible for their own taxes, tools, licences and insurance where needed.

4. Posting and doing quests

  • Describe the quest honestly, including the place, the work, any safety issues and the pay.
  • Applying is an offer to do the quest as described. Once the poster picks you and you confirm, you’re hired.
  • Workers check in, take before and after photos where asked, and mark the quest finished. Posters confirm it’s done, or ask once for it to be finished.
  • Show up when agreed. No-shows, late cancellations and problems are kept on your trust record and shown to others.

5. Not allowed

No quests or listings for anything illegal or unsafe, including weapons, drugs, alcohol or tobacco for minors, animals for sale, recalled items, adult services, or anything that needs a licence the worker doesn’t have. No harassment, discrimination, scams, or taking a quest’s payment outside QuestBoard to avoid the fee. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

6. Payments

  • Two ways to pay. A poster chooses “Pay in the app” or “Pay each other directly” (cash, Venmo and so on). Payment protection, refunds, credit and disputes over money apply only to quests paid in the app.
  • When the card is charged. For quests paid in the app, the poster’s card is held when they pick a worker and charged when the quest starts (or 6 days after the pick, if sooner). QuestBoard holds the money until the quest is done.
  • Our fee. Posters pay the listed price, with no fee. QuestBoard keeps 10% of the worker’s pay as its fee, which covers card processing, payment protection and support.
  • Paying workers. After a quest is completed, the worker’s pay is held for 7 days (3 days for Trusted workers) so problems can be raised, then sent to their Stripe account. Workers need a Stripe payout account to be paid in the app, and Stripe’s terms apply to it.
  • Payments partner. Payments are processed by Stripe. QuestBoard never sees full card or bank numbers.

7. Cancelling

  • There are no cancellation fees.
  • Cancelled before the card is charged: the hold is dropped and nothing is charged.
  • Cancelled after the charge: the poster gets the whole amount back, as QuestBoard credit (the default) or to their card. Refunds to a card are limited to 3 in any 30 days; after that, money stays as credit until another is available.
  • Cancelling a hired quest late (less than 24 hours before) is recorded on the canceller’s trust record.

8. QuestBoard credit

Credit is money returned from a cancelled or disputed quest. It’s used first on your next quest paid in the app. It doesn’t expire, can’t be transferred to anyone else, and can be refunded only to the card it came from, within about 6 months of the original charge (Stripe’s limit). It isn’t a bank account and earns no interest.

9. Disputes

  • If something goes wrong, either side can open a dispute: the poster after the worker finishes, either side up to 7 days after a quest is done, or a worker who was removed.
  • Both sides give their account and photos and can agree on an outcome: completed, partly completed (the worker gets 25%, 50% or 75% of the pay), not completed, or no one’s fault.
  • If it isn’t settled within 3 days, QuestBoard decides, and that decision is final within QuestBoard. Money is held while a dispute is open. If a worker has already been paid and the decision says they’re owed less, the difference is taken back from their Stripe account.

10. The market

The market lets people list items for sale to others nearby. QuestBoard doesn’t handle market payments or check items. Buyers and sellers deal with each other directly and at their own risk, and the “Not allowed” rules apply.

11. Photos and content

You keep ownership of what you post. You give QuestBoard permission to store, show and use it to run the app (for example, showing your place photos to other explorers, or your quest photos to the person you’re working with and in a dispute). Only post photos you have the right to share, and don’t photograph people without their permission.

12. Safety

Use good judgement when meeting people and entering homes. QuestBoard checks ages and shows trust records, but doesn’t run background checks unless a badge says so, and can’t guarantee anyone’s conduct. In an emergency, call 911.

13. Responsibility

QuestBoard is provided as is. As far as the law allows, QuestBoard isn’t responsible for the work done on quests, for injuries or damage that happen during them, or for dealings between users. Our total responsibility to you is limited to the fees QuestBoard received from your quests in the past 12 months.

14. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time (see how). We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put others at risk. Money you’re owed is still paid, unless it’s part of a dispute or fraud.

15. Changes

We may update these terms. We’ll change the date above and tell you in the app if a change is significant. Using QuestBoard after a change means you accept it.
