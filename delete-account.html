Delete your account

In the app

Open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. It shows what will be deleted, anything you need to finish first, and what happens to any money, then asks you to type DELETE to confirm. It happens straight away.

By email

Can’t open the app? Email adamayers@myquestboard.com from the email address on your My Quest Board account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll confirm it’s you and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Before you go

  • Finish or cancel any quest that’s in progress, and close any open dispute, so the other person isn’t left out. The app won’t delete the account until then.
  • Your open quests are cancelled and your applications withdrawn for you.
  • Any My Quest Board credit goes back to the card it came from. Credit older than about 6 months can’t go back to a card, so use it on a quest first.
  • Workers: any pay you’re owed is still sent to your Stripe account. Your Stripe account itself is managed by Stripe.

What’s deleted

  • Your sign-in, email address, phone number and date of birth.
  • Your profile and worker profile. Others see “Deleted user” where your name was.
  • Your exploration progress, places visited, plans, saved properties and settings.
  • Your market listings and their photos, and the cards saved for paying.

What’s kept

  • Payment and tax records, which Stripe and My Quest Board are required to keep for several years.
  • Quests, messages, reviews, disputes and quest photos that other people were part of, without your name.
  • Place photos you shared on the map, without your name.
