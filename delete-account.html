Delete your account

How to ask

Email adamayers@myquestboard.com from the email address on your QuestBoard account, with the subject “Delete my QuestBoard account”. We’ll confirm it’s you and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

A button to do it yourself in the app is coming.

Before you go

  • Finish or cancel any quests you’re part of. A quest that’s in progress or under dispute has to be closed first, so the other person isn’t left out.
  • If you have QuestBoard credit, refund it to your card first (Settings, then QuestBoard credit). Credit can’t be paid out after the account is gone.
  • Workers: any pay you’re owed is still sent to your Stripe account. Your Stripe account itself is managed by Stripe.

What’s deleted

  • Your account, email address, date of birth and phone number.
  • Your profile, worker profile, photos, messages, market listings, saved places and exploration progress.

What’s kept

  • Payment and tax records, which Stripe and QuestBoard are required to keep for several years.
  • Reviews you left and quests that other people were part of, without your name.
