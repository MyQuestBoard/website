QuestBoard

Explore Ohio. Earn on the side.

QuestBoard turns getting out and about into a game, with a board of small paid jobs, called quests, posted by people near you.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

Uncover the map

Everywhere you go lifts the fog. Parks, trails, museums and hidden gems earn you XP and ranks.

A quest board in every county

Yard work, deliveries, moving help, tech help, pet care and more, posted by people nearby.

Paid safely in the app

The poster’s payment is held before you start and sent to your bank after. No chasing anyone for cash.

Trust you can see

Reviews, reliability, check-ins, before and after photos, and a fair way to sort out problems.

For people who need a hand

Post what you need and what you’ll pay. Posting is free, you pay only the listed price, and your card isn’t charged until the quest starts. If it’s cancelled, you get it all back.

For people who want to earn

Find quests near you, apply, and get paid through the app after the work is done. QuestBoard keeps 10% of the pay to cover payment protection, card costs and support.
